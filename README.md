# Grok vs Other AI

Two local chess engines in the browser, plus human vs Grok.

- **Watch bots:** Grok (aggressive) vs Other AI (quieter).
- **Play vs Grok:** click pieces. Choose who is White (White moves first).
- Board colors and cburnett pieces follow the usual Lichess look.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Needs internet once for chess rules and piece images.
